CapsPlan for Home Assistant

Sign in to connect your medications

  1. Click “Sign in with Apple ID” and sign in with the Apple ID that CapsPlan syncs with.
  2. If Apple offers “Keep me signed in”, tick it.
  3. Copy the token shown below and paste it into the CapsPlan setup in Home Assistant right away.

Your sign-in token

Use the token right away and only once. Do not reload this page afterwards; if the setup says the token is invalid, sign in again here.

This page only talks to Apple. The token is shown here in your browser and is not sent anywhere else. It grants access to CapsPlan's data in your iCloud only, never to other iCloud data.

Connecting several people? Sign in with each Apple ID in a private browser window, so Apple doesn't reuse the previous sign-in.

Accounts with Advanced Data Protection turned on cannot sign in here (an Apple restriction for apps outside of Apple's own).
