# CapsPlan for Home Assistant

Bring your medications from [CapsPlan](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779642344) into Home Assistant: next dose, open doses, stock and tapering dose as sensors — read from your own iCloud, free and open source.

> **Work in progress.** The integration is being tested right now and will be published here shortly. The sign-in page for the setup is already live: <https://dxdotm.github.io/capsplan-home-assistant/>
